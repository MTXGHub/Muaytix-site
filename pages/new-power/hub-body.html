What is New Power Muay Thai?

New Power Muay Thai is Rajadamnern Stadium’s Wednesday fight programme, built around traditional five-round Muay Thai.

Unlike a three-round format, the fighters have more time to establish distance, rhythm and intent before the tactical contest develops through the later rounds.

The crowd follows those changes closely. Reactions are shaped not only by individual punches, kicks or knockdowns, but by shifts in timing, control, scoring and momentum.

Traditional sarama music accompanies the fights and remains part of the rhythm of the evening.

Traditional five-round Muay Thai

New Power represents the long game of Muay Thai.

The opening rounds can establish distance and rhythm. Fighters and corners then respond to what is happening in the ring, while the tactical pressure develops across the five-round structure.

That gives the later stages meaning that cannot always be understood by looking only for constant attacks or knockdowns.

For someone watching traditional Muay Thai for the first time, the key is to notice how the fight changes from round to round.

A fighter who appears patient early may be establishing range, reading an opponent or waiting for the moment when the balance of the contest begins to shift.

Wednesday night Muay Thai in Bangkok

If you are looking for a Wednesday fight night in Bangkok, New Power is the regular Wednesday Muay Thai event at Rajadamnern Stadium.

The audience includes a strong local following, with spectators reacting closely to changes in scoring and momentum.

Legal betting activity is also part of the traditional stadium environment and can be particularly visible around the LEO seating area.

It is part of the wider fight-night culture rather than the purpose of the event itself.

New Power Muay Thai questions

What day is New Power Muay Thai?

New Power Muay Thai takes place on Wednesday evenings at Rajadamnern Stadium in Bangkok.

What time does New Power Muay Thai start?

The event normally starts at 18:00, with doors opening at 17:00.

How many rounds are New Power Muay Thai fights?

New Power uses the traditional five-round Muay Thai format.

How many fights are normally on the programme?

New Power normally features eight or nine bouts. The confirmed number can vary by event.

Is New Power the same as Rajadamnern Knockout?

No. New Power uses traditional five-round Muay Thai, while Rajadamnern Knockout uses a shorter three-round format.

Is New Power suitable for someone watching Muay Thai for the first time?

Yes. A first-time visitor does not need to understand every part of Muay Thai scoring. The five-round structure makes it possible to watch the rhythm and tactical contest develop as the fight progresses.

What is sarama?

Sarama is the traditional music performed during Muay Thai bouts. It follows the rhythm of the contest and is part of the traditional stadium environment.

Can I arrive after the fights have started?

Yes. The stadium gates remain open during the event, so guests may enter after the programme has begun.
