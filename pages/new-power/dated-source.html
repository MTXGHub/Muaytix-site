What to expect from New Power Muay Thai

New Power is built around traditional five-round Muay Thai rather than the shorter three-round format used by some other Rajadamnern events.

The opening rounds can establish distance, rhythm and intent before the tactical contest develops through the later stages.

Fighters and corners respond between rounds, while scoring and momentum influence the way each contest develops.

Traditional sarama music accompanies the fights throughout the evening.

New Power normally features eight or nine bouts, although the confirmed number for an individual event can vary.

A traditional Wednesday night at Rajadamnern

A strong local audience is an important part of New Power.

Spectators follow changes in timing, control, scoring and momentum, so the reaction inside the stadium often tells a first-time visitor that something important in the fight has changed.

Legal betting activity is also part of the traditional stadium environment and can be particularly visible around the LEO seating area.

It is one part of the wider Wednesday fight night atmosphere rather than the reason to attend.
