H2
The view from Section 10 Rajadamnern Stadium

Headline
Face the ring, not the side of it.

Body
LEO looks straight at the front of the ring. Club Class, next door, looks towards the sides. You share the same elevation and the same rows, so the difference is direction, not height.

That direction matters more than most first-time visitors expect. The blue corner is directly in front of the section, the fighters enter past it, and the commentary team sits here. The camera gantry is in LEO, so the main broadcast cameras shoot from this side because it gives the clearest read of the whole canvas. When the referee raises the winner's hand at the end of a bout, the fighters are facing LEO.

Detail
MuayTix recommends around Row D and above for the fullest elevated view across the ring. Front row gives a flatter, closer, almost eye-level view. Both are good seats. The choice is yours on the night.

H2
Is LEO Section unassigned seating? Yes, with a seat guaranteed

Headline
Unassigned means you choose. It never means you stand.

Body
LEO is concrete bench seating in Section 10, with 300 seats and strict control of ticket numbers. Every ticket has a seat waiting. There is no standing area and no risk of arriving to find the section full.

Unassigned seating gives you control after you walk in. You do not need to study a seat map or guess which row will suit you. Doors open one hour before the first bout. Arrive at opening if you want the full choice of rows, including front row. Arrive later if you are not fussed about the exact row, because your seat is still there.

Detail
If a better position opens up during the night, you can move to it. For groups, that flexibility is the reason LEO works so well: you sit together wherever you like.

[BUILD: FAQ schema. Each answer is written to stand alone when quoted.]

H2
LEO Section seating questions

Q: What is the LEO Section at Rajadamnern Stadium?
A: The Rajadamnern LEO Section is Section 10, main-arena seating that faces the front of the ring at exactly the same elevation as Club Class. It has 300 unassigned seats, in-section food and drink service, and the strongest local atmosphere in the stadium. Tickets are 1,500 THB.

Q: Is LEO Section the same as Section 10?
A: Yes. LEO Section and Section 10 are the same section. Rajadamnern Stadium numbers its sections, and LEO is the name used for Section 10.

Q: Was the LEO Section called Second Class?
A: Yes. Older guides and some ticket listings call Section 10 Second Class, 2nd Class, LEO Second Class or LEO Class. They all mean the same section. LEO Section is the current name.

Q: Is LEO assigned seating?
A: No. LEO is unassigned seating. You pick your position within Section 10 when you arrive, and you can move to another free position during the event.

Q: Do I have to stand in LEO?
A: No. Every LEO ticket guarantees a seat. LEO is concrete bench seating with strict control of ticket numbers, and there is no standing area.

Q: Do I need to arrive early to get a seat in LEO?
A: No. Your seat is guaranteed whenever you arrive. Arriving when doors open, one hour before the first bout, gives you the widest choice of position, including front row.

Q: Is LEO the same level as Club Class?
A: Yes. LEO has exactly the same elevation and the same number of rows as Club Class. LEO faces the front of the ring, while Club Class looks towards the sides.

Q: What view do you get from LEO?
A: A front-facing view across the whole ring from an elevated position, with the blue corner directly in front of the section. The camera gantry and commentary team are positioned in LEO.

Q: Which rows are best in LEO?
A: MuayTix recommends around Row D and above for the fullest elevated view. Front row gives a closer, almost eye-level view of the ring. Both are good positions.

Q: Is LEO the betting section?
A: On Wednesday New Power, Thursday Petchyindee and Sunday Kiatpetch, the local legal betting crowd gathers in LEO and creates a high-energy atmosphere. On other nights LEO is simply the section local Muay Thai fans prefer.

Q: Do I have to take part in betting?
A: No. You sit alongside the atmosphere and watch the fight. Nobody expects you to take part.

Q: Is free-flow beer included in LEO?
A: Only on Saturday RWS Rajadamnern World Series events, when free-flow beer is included with the LEO ticket at the normal 1,500 THB price. On other nights, servers bring drinks to the section and you pay as you order.

Q: Is LEO suitable for children and families?
A: Yes. There is no age restriction, and the open bench seating lets families and groups sit together wherever they like.
