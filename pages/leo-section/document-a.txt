[answer.h2]
What is the LEO Section at Rajadamnern Stadium?

[answer.p1]
The LEO Section is Section 10, an elevated section facing the front of the ring.

[answer.p2]
It has unassigned seating, but every ticket guarantees a seat. You choose your position within the section when you arrive.

[answer.p3]
The section is where the broadcast cameras and commentary team are positioned, helping create a strong view across the entire ring.

[answer.p4]
On Wednesday New Power, Thursday Petchyindee and Sunday Kiatpetch, LEO is also where the local betting crowd gathers.

[view.h2]
A front-facing view of the whole ring

[view.p1]
LEO looks directly towards the front of the ring.

[view.p2]
Its tier, elevation and row configuration are effectively the same as Club Class next door, giving you a clear elevated view across the canvas.

[view.p3]
The principal broadcast cameras and commentary team are positioned in LEO. This is one reason the section offers such a strong front-facing perspective.

[view.p4]
You can follow the movement across the ring, watch the corners between rounds and see the fighters enter before the bout begins.

[view.p5]
MuayTix generally recommends around Row D and above for the optimal overall sightline. This is a viewing preference, not a warning that the lower rows are poor seats.

[atmosphere.h2]
The section where the atmosphere lives

[atmosphere.p1]
On Wednesday, Thursday and Sunday traditional Muay Thai nights, LEO is where local Thai fight fans and the legal betting crowd gather.

[atmosphere.p2]
Betting uses hand signals and creates a distinctive high-energy atmosphere as the crowd responds to changes in the fight.

[atmosphere.p3]
You do not have to take part in betting.

[atmosphere.p4]
The area is organised so visitors can sit alongside the activity, watch what is happening and experience the atmosphere without actively participating.

[atmosphere.p5]
The betting atmosphere applies to these three traditional fight nights only. It should not be applied to every night of the week.

[position.h2]
Flexible seating, with a seat guaranteed

[position.p1]
LEO is unassigned seating, which means you choose where to sit within Section 10.

[position.p2]
Every ticket guarantees a seat. Unassigned does not mean standing, and it does not mean that you risk being left without a place.

[position.p3]
Arriving early is not required to secure a seat. It simply gives you more choice of position, including the possibility of choosing the front row when it is available.

[position.p4]
MuayTix generally recommends around Row D and above for the clearest overall sightline.
