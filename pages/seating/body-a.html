Rajadamnern Stadium Seating: Choose the Right Seats for Your Fight Night

Rajadamnern Stadium has four public seat classes: Ringside, Club Class, LEO Section and Third Class. There are no bad views in the building. The choice is about how you want to experience the night: right beside the ring, elevated with a numbered seat, in the middle of the atmosphere, or looking over the whole arena.

At a glance

The four seat classes

Ringside

Sections 3 to 7 · Floor level, main arena

Assigned seat, allocated from the front row backwards

The ring is a few metres away. You hear the strikes and the corner instructions.

Feels like: a working fight-night floor. Corners, officials and broadcast crews are all around you.

Suits: fight fans who want maximum proximity, groups who want to sit together close to the ring, photographers who want close-range angles

2,500 THB

See Ringside seats and view

Club Class

Sections 8 and 9 · Elevated tier, main arena

Assigned, numbered seat. Staff can walk you to it.

Looks down across the whole ring, with the walkouts and corners in view.

Feels like: busy and atmospheric on a full night, and the easiest section for families and groups. Toilets, food and drink are close by.

Suits: first-time guests, families with children, anyone who wants a dependable all-round choice. Around 55 per cent of MuayTix guests choose Club Class.

1,800 THB

See Club Class seats and view

LEO Section

Section 10 · Elevated tier, facing the front of the ring

Unassigned seating, seat guaranteed. Choose where to sit when you arrive.

The broadcast cameras and commentary team sit here, which tells you how good the front-facing view is.

Feels like: where the atmosphere lives. On Wednesday, Thursday and Sunday it is the home of local Thai fight fans and the betting crowd.

Suits: couples, solo travellers and groups who like choosing their own spot, repeat visitors, anyone who wants the local fight-night feel

1,500 THB

See the LEO Section and view

Third Class

Section 11 · Upper tier, the highest point in the stadium

Unassigned seating, seat guaranteed

A wide view over the whole arena: ring, walkout, corners and crowd in one picture.

Feels like: being inside Rajadamnern on a big night. Third Class opens as the stadium fills, so it is at its best when the building is full.

Suits: guests who want the full-stadium perspective, repeat visitors after a different angle, photographers who want a high, wide shot
