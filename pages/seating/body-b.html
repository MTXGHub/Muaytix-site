Which Rajadamnern seats are best? It depends what you want

There is no single best seat at Rajadamnern. There is a best seat for the night you want.

Best for being as close as possible

Ringside. Sections 3 to 7 sit on the floor of the main arena, a few metres from the ropes. If you want to feel the impact of every strike, this is the section. Sections 3 and 4 sit either side of the fighter entrance walkway, so they are the closest seats to the walkouts. Ringside seats at Rajadamnern

Best for a numbered seat and an easy night

Club Class. Sections 8 and 9 are elevated, every seat is numbered, and facilities are a short walk away. It is the most popular choice with MuayTix guests and the most straightforward option for families. Club Class, Sections 8 and 9

Best for atmosphere

LEO Section. Section 10 faces the front of the ring and is where the local fight fans gather on the three traditional Muay Thai nights: Wednesday, Thursday and Sunday. It is also where the broadcast cameras sit, so the view straight down the ring is the one you see on television. LEO Section, Section 10

Best for seeing the whole stadium

Third Class. Section 11 is the top of the building and shows you the entire arena at once. It opens when demand fills the main arena, which is exactly when the atmosphere is at its strongest. Third Class, Section 11

The view

The view from each seat class

Ringside

Ring-level and close. You see the fighters at eye height, with the ropes, corners and officials as part of the picture. Sound is as much of the experience as sight.

Club Class

An elevated view that takes in the full canvas. From Row D and above you sit clearly above the people moving along the walkway and look down on the whole ring.

LEO Section

Front-facing and elevated, on the same tier as Club Class. This is the broadcast angle. You watch the fight square-on and can follow movement across the ring easily.

Third Class

High and wide. From Row D and above you see the ring, the walkout, the main arena and the crowd as one scene.

Rows

Rows: how MuayTix places you

The section decides the experience. The row decides how well you see it. MuayTix places guests with that in mind.

  • RingsideAllocated from the front row backwards. Front row is the closest and most immersive position and most guests ask for it, so we allocate it first unless you tell us otherwise.
  • Club ClassWe recommend Row D and above. It lifts you clearly above the walkway eye line for an elevated view of the whole ring.
  • LEO SectionSeating is unassigned, so you choose. We suggest around Row D and above for the best overall sightline. Arriving earlier simply gives you more choice of position, including the front row when it is free.
  • Third ClassRows A to C face a safety fence. Row D and above gives you the clearest view over the arena.
Seating explained

Assigned and unassigned seating explained

Ringside and Club Class are assigned. Your seat number is on your ticket.

LEO Section and Third Class are unassigned. That means you choose where to sit within the section when you arrive. It does not mean seating is limited and it does not mean anyone stands. There are no standing areas at Rajadamnern Stadium, and every ticket guarantees a seat. How unassigned seating works in the LEO Section
