What is Rajadamnern Knockout?

Rajadamnern Knockout brings fast three-round fights to one of Bangkok’s historic Muay Thai stadiums. Thai fighters meet international challengers in a high-energy evening that is easy to follow, even if this is your first live Muay Thai event.

Each event usually features seven three-round bouts, with knees, elbows and clinching under Muay Thai rules. Rajadamnern promotes the series around an advertised 80% knockout rate.

New to Muay Thai? You are in the right place.

You do not need to understand scoring, fighter rankings or stadium traditions before you arrive. The three-round format is easy to follow, and the action starts from the opening bell.

  • Fast format

    Seven three-round bouts keep the night moving.

  • Thai traditions

    See Wai Kru and hear live traditional music.

  • Historic setting

    Watch inside Rajadamnern Stadium, one of Bangkok’s best-known Muay Thai venues.

What to expect at Rajadamnern Knockout

Rajadamnern Knockout combines modern three-round competition with the traditions that make Muay Thai unique.

Expect Wai Kru before the bouts, live traditional Muay Thai music, knees, elbows and clinching under Muay Thai rules, plus Thai fighters and international challengers competing inside Rajadamnern Stadium.

There is usually an interval around 8:00 pm, followed by the Rajadamnern Immersive Dome Experience at around 8:10 pm.

Fights start on time. Fight cards and later running times can change.

The Rajadamnern Dome Experience

After the interval, the stadium roof becomes an immersive visual show celebrating Muay Thai history and culture. It usually begins at around 8:10 pm.

The first fight starts on time. Later timings can vary with the length of the bouts.
