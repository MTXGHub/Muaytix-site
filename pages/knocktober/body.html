Meet the headline fighters

Petchsila Wor. AurachaRajadamnern Super Bantamweight champion

A Thai southpaw and proven stadium-level champion. Petchsila enters as the home champion against Japanese RISE titleholder Koki Osaki.

Koki OsakiJapanese RISE Bantamweight champion

One of Japan’s leading lighter-weight fighters, Osaki arrives with proven success in RWS rules and an interim Super Bantamweight title.

Egor BikrevRajadamnern Lightweight champion

The Russian champion made history by winning the title with the fastest knockout in RWS history. He now defends against former RISE champion Kan Nakamura.

Kan NakamuraFormer RISE Lightweight champion

A high-output Japanese contender with a strong knockout record and a dangerous knee-strike game.
