Who you are booking with

The team behind your ticket

Here is what booking with MuayTix means.

Tickets from the stadium

Every ticket comes from the stadium's own allocation. Nothing second-hand, nothing marked up by a middleman.

Seated together

Book two or more tickets in one order and you sit together.

Pay in your own currency

Multi-currency checkout shows your exact total before you pay. Secure payment through Stripe.

Your phone is your ticket

A named QR e-ticket arrives by email. Show it at the gate and walk in. No printing, no collection.

Real people, every night

The MuayTix team is on WhatsApp seven days a week and on standby at every event. If something goes wrong at the gate, message us.

If we cannot provide the tickets you booked, you choose: an alternative seat class or a full refund the same day.
