First time?

You do not need to know the rules

Every bout has a clear winner, the crowd tells you when something big happens, and the night runs two to three hours. Dress is casual, children are welcome, and you can arrive after the first bell.

Is it suitable for families?

Yes. Children are welcome at every event. Club Class is the easiest section with young ones because the seats are assigned and there is more room.

What time should I arrive?

Doors open about an hour before the first bout. Arriving 30 minutes early gives you time to get in, find your section and settle down. Each event page shows the exact times.

Can I arrive after the fights have started?

Yes. The gates stay open through the evening, so you can enter at any time after the first bell.

What should I wear?

Whatever you are comfortable in. There is no dress code. Closed shoes are a good idea if you are sitting Ringside.

How long does a fight night last?

Two to three hours, depending on the event. Most nights finish between 9:00 PM and 10:00 PM.

The venue

The world's first Muay Thai stadium

Rajadamnern Stadium opened in 1945 and still hosts professional Muay Thai every night of the week. Modern lights, screens and a dome show now sit inside the original ring and tiered seating.
