The event

Bangkok's biggest Saturday night in Muay Thai

On Saturday 26 September, Rajadamnern Stadium hosts seven professional bouts with Thai and international fighters on the same card. Regular bouts run three rounds, championship contests run five, and the scores go up on the screens after every round, so you can follow every fight without knowing the sport.

The full arena production, the dome show and a mixed Thai and international crowd make Saturday the most complete night to see live Muay Thai in Bangkok.

Seating

Choose where you sit

Every seat class watches the same seven bouts. The difference is how close you are and the kind of crowd around you. The seat classes available for 26 September are shown in the booking section below.

Ringside

Closest to the ring

Ringside offers the closest viewing positions, with the strongest sense of impact as the fighters exchange strikes in the ring. Seats are assigned by the stadium when you book.

Best for: Guests who want proximity above everything else

Book Ringside

Club Class

Elevated and comfortable

Club Class provides elevated, assigned seating, clear views of the ring and a comfortable balance between atmosphere and viewing position.

Best for: First visits, couples and anyone wanting a clear, unobstructed view

Book Club Class

LEO Section

Where the atmosphere lives

The LEO Section is unassigned seating, but every guest is guaranteed a seat. The elevation and viewing position are the same as Club Class. It is popular with local supporters and is known for its lively atmosphere, placing guests close to the rhythm and reaction of the stadium.

Best for: Guests who want local energy and crowd atmosphere

Book LEO Section

Third Class

Traditional stadium viewing

Traditional stadium viewing with a full-ring perspective and strong value, offering a direct connection to the atmosphere of live Muay Thai. Seating is unassigned and every ticket guarantees a seat.

Best for: Guests seeking the most traditional stadium view

Book Third Class
