First time?

You do not need to know the rules to enjoy the night

Most of the people in the stadium on any given night have never watched Muay Thai before. You will see the ritual before each bout, hear the live music that speeds up as the fight does, and feel the crowd react to every kick and clinch. It is a night out in Bangkok first and a sporting event second. Dress is casual, the stadium sells food and drink, and you can arrive after the first bell if your evening runs late.

Two to three hours

Most nights finish between 9:00 PM and 10:00 PM, so you can still eat before or after.

Families welcome

Children are welcome at every event. Club Class is the easiest section with young ones.

Easy to follow

Every bout has a clear winner, the crowd tells you when something big has happened, and Saturday nights show the scores on screen after every round.

Seating

Every seat watches the same fights

The difference is how close you are and the kind of crowd around you.

Ringside

The closest seats to the ring. For guests who want proximity above everything else.

Club Class

Elevated, assigned seats with a clear view. The easiest all-round choice, and the one we recommend for first visits, couples and families.

LEO Section

Unassigned seating at the same height as Club Class, where the local supporters sit. For guests who want the atmosphere.

Third Class

The lowest priced way in, with a full view of the stadium from the top tier. For guests on a budget who want the traditional stadium experience.

The venue

The world's first Muay Thai stadium

Rajadamnern Stadium opened in 1945 on Ratchadamnoen Nok Road in central Bangkok and has hosted professional Muay Thai ever since. Today it combines the original ring and tiered seating with modern lighting, screens and a dome projection show. It is the stadium every Thai fighter wants to win at, and the one visitors remember.

Your questions

Quick answers

Can I book on the day?

Yes. We sell tickets right up to the start of the event while there is availability. Popular seat classes can sell out earlier on busy nights, so if you know your date, book it.

Can I change my date or seat after booking?

The stadium's system does not allow changes once a ticket is issued. If your event is still some way off, message us on WhatsApp and we will do what we can.

What should I wear?

Whatever you are comfortable in. There is no dress code. Closed shoes are a good idea if you are sitting Ringside.

What time should I arrive?

Doors open an hour or so before the first bout. Arriving 30 minutes early gives you time to get in, find your section and settle down. Check your event page for the exact times.
