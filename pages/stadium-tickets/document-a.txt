# ============================== BLOCK 10: FAQ ==============================

[faq.h2]
Rajadamnern Stadium Ticket Questions

[faq.q1]
How much do Rajadamnern Stadium tickets cost?

[faq.a1]
Ringside is 2,500 THB, Club Class 1,800 THB, LEO Section 1,500 THB and Third Class 1,000 THB. Prices are set by seat class, not by night. Select your date in the booking section to see the live price in your currency.

[faq.q2]
Is MuayTix an official seller of Rajadamnern Stadium tickets?

[faq.a2]
Yes. MuayTix is the international booking partner of Rajadamnern Stadium. Every ticket comes from the stadium's own allocation.

[faq.q3]
Can I buy tickets at Rajadamnern Stadium on the day?

[faq.a3]
Sometimes. The stadium does not hold back seats for walk-up guests, so on busy nights the only option left may be Third Class, which can sell out by midday on RWS Saturdays. Book online if you know the night you want.

[faq.q4]
What time does Muay Thai start at Rajadamnern Stadium?

[faq.a4]
The first fight is at 7:00 PM on Monday, Tuesday and Friday, 6:00 PM on Wednesday, Thursday and Sunday, and 7:10 PM on Saturday. Doors open about an hour earlier.

[faq.q5]
Which seat class should I choose for a first visit?

[faq.a5]
Club Class. Elevated, assigned seats at ring height give the clearest view in the stadium, and just over half of MuayTix guests choose it.

[faq.q6]
Will our seats be together?

[faq.a6]
Yes. Two or more tickets booked in one order are always seated together. The quantity you can select is the number of seats available side by side for that date and class.

[faq.q7]
Can I pick my exact seat or row?

[faq.a7]
Exact seat selection is not offered as standard. The MuayTix team is inside the stadium regularly and allocates the best available positions in your seat class at the time you book. In Club Class, LEO Section and Third Class the second to fourth rows usually give a better view than the front row.

[faq.q8]
How will I receive my ticket?

[faq.a8]
By email, as a QR code. Show it on your phone at a box-office window at the stadium. Check your junk folder if you cannot see it, or message MuayTix on WhatsApp.

[faq.q9]
Which currencies can I pay in?

[faq.a9]
GBP, USD, EUR, AUD, CNY or THB. Stripe Checkout shows the payment methods available for your country and device before you pay. MuayTix adds no booking fee and no currency-conversion fee.

[faq.q10]
Can you help with group bookings?

[faq.a10]
Yes. Contact the MuayTix team by email or WhatsApp and one person will handle your group from first enquiry to fight night.

[faq.q11]
What if my first-choice seat class is sold out?

[faq.a11]
Book the best class still available. Every seat class has a clear view of the ring, and the night is about the atmosphere, the crowd and the fights, not the seat label.

[faq.q12]
Is Rajadamnern Stadium suitable for families and first-time guests?

[faq.a12]
Yes. Children are welcome at every event, and you do not need to know the rules to enjoy the night. Club Class is the easiest section with young children because seats are assigned.
