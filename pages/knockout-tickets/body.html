Rajadamnern Knockout at Rajadamnern Stadium

Rajadamnern Knockout is a fast three-round Muay Thai event at Rajadamnern Stadium in Bangkok.

The event takes place every Monday, Tuesday and Friday. Most nights include seven bouts, with doors opening at 6:00 pm and the first fight beginning at 7:00 pm.

Most events finish around 9:00 pm to 9:30 pm. The finish time can vary with the length of the bouts.
