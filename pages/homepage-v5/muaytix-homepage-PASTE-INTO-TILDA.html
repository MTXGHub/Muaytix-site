Choose your seats at Rajadamnern Stadium

All four public seat classes watch the same fights. What changes is your distance from the ring, viewing height and the crowd around you.

  • Ringside

    Closest to the ring

    Assigned seating beside the action for guests who want proximity above everything else.

    See Ringside seating

  • Club Class

    Elevated, assigned seating

    A clear view across the ring with assigned seats, making Club Class an easy choice for many first-time visitors and groups.

    See Club Class seating

  • LEO Section

    Where the local atmosphere is strongest

    Unassigned seating at the same general level as Club Class, with a strong local following and a more involved stadium atmosphere.

    See the LEO Section

  • Third Class

    The lowest-priced public seat class

    An elevated full-ring perspective from the outer tier when Third Class is available for the selected event.

Rajadamnern Stadium, Bangkok

Rajadamnern Stadium opened in 1945 and remains one of Bangkok’s historic homes of professional Muay Thai.

The stadium is on Ratchadamnoen Nok Road in central Bangkok and hosts different Muay Thai events throughout the week.
