Ringside: Sections 3 to 7, on the floor around the ring

[ringside.h2]
Ringside Sections 3 to 7

[ringside.p1]
Ringside seats are located on the floor around the ring in Sections 3 to 7.

[ringside.p2]
The ring is only a few metres from the audience.

[ringside.p3]
The raised fighter entrance walkway runs between and through Sections 3 and 4. These sections are closest to the formal fighter entrance route and have a smaller, more intimate layout than Sections 5, 6 and 7.

[ringside.p4]
Ringside is assigned seating.

[clubclass.h2]
Club Class Sections 8 and 9

[clubclass.p1]
Club Class is located on the elevated main-arena tier in Sections 8 and 9.

[clubclass.p2]
The elevated position gives guests a broad view down across the ring.

[clubclass.p3]
Club Class is assigned seating, and every guest receives a numbered seat.

[clubclass.p4]
It is particularly suitable for families, groups and first-time visitors who want a straightforward seating arrangement.

[leo.h2]
LEO Section 10

[leo.p1]
LEO is Section 10, located on the elevated main-arena tier and facing the front of the ring.

[leo.p2]
Its tier, elevation and row configuration are effectively the same as Club Class next door.

[leo.p3]
LEO has unassigned seating, but every ticket guarantees a seat.

[leo.p4]
On Wednesday New Power, Thursday Petchyindee and Sunday Kiatpetch, the local betting crowd gathers in this section.

[leo.link]

[third.h2]
Third Class Section 11

[third.p1]
Third Class is Section 11, located at the highest point of Rajadamnern Stadium.

[third.p2]
It gives guests a wide view across the ring, entrance walkway, crowd and arena.

[third.p3]
Third Class has unassigned seating, but every ticket guarantees a seat.

[third.p4]
Rows A to C face a safety fence. MuayTix generally recommends Row D and above for the clearest overall perspective.

[third.p5]
Third Class opens according to demand and is highly seasonal.
