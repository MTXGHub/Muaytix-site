    Rajadamnern's flagship Saturday night.

    Thai and international fighters compete in predominantly three-round contests with open scoring and major-event production, while Wai Kru and traditional Muay Thai remain part of the night.

    Saturday · Doors from 6:00 PM

    Explore RWS

  • Rajadamnern Knockout

    7 fights. 3 rounds. 80% knockout rate.

Where you sit

Choose your seats at Rajadamnern Stadium

Every seat class watches the same fights. What changes is how close you are to the ring, how high you sit, and the crowd around you.

  • Ringside

    Closest to the ring

    Floor level, sections 3 to 7. Close enough to hear the corner shouting instructions. Your seat number is printed on your ticket.

    See Ringside seating

  • Club Class

    Elevated view of the entire ring

    Sections 8 and 9, one tier up. You see the whole ring and the whole crowd. Your seat number is printed on your ticket, and a group booked in one order sits together.

    See Club Class seating

  • LEO Section

    Where the atmosphere lives

    Section 10, the same tier as Club Class. This is where the Thai regulars sit and where the noise comes from. Seating is open within the section.

    See the LEO Section

  • Third Class

    360 degree view of the action

    Section 11, the upper tier, and normally the lowest-priced seat on sale. Seating is open within the section. Third Class opens on selected nights only.

    See Third Class seating

Muay Thai ticket prices and booking questions

What is the Muay Thai Bangkok tickets price?

Prices depend on the event and the seat class. The live calendar shows the exact price for the date you choose, in your own currency, before you pay.

Are cheap Muay Thai tickets Bangkok available?

The lowest-priced option varies by event and availability. Third Class is normally the lowest-priced seat class when it is open for sale.

Are Thai boxing tickets the same as Muay Thai tickets?

Yes. Thai boxing is simply the English name for Muay Thai, so Thai boxing Bangkok tickets, Thai boxing tickets Bangkok and Bangkok Thai boxing tickets all mean the same thing: a seat at a Rajadamnern fight night.

Do Muay Thai stadium tickets work at any stadium in Bangkok?

No. Muay Thai stadium Bangkok tickets are sold for one stadium and one night. Bangkok Muay Thai stadium tickets bought here are for Rajadamnern on the date you choose. If you are buying Muay Thai boxing stadium tickets anywhere else, check which stadium they are for before you pay.

Are Muay Thai fight tickets Bangkok sold for one night only?

Yes. Bangkok Muay Thai fight tickets admit you to one named fight night. Muay Thai fight Bangkok tickets are never a general pass, so choose your date first.

I have seen it written as tickets Muay Thai Bangkok. Is that the same?

Yes. Ticket Muay Thai Bangkok, tickets Muay Thai Bangkok, Muay Thai ticket Bangkok and Muay Thai boxing Bangkok tickets are the same search written in different word orders. They all lead to the same thing: a seat at Rajadamnern on a night you choose.

Do I need to print my ticket?

No. Your QR ticket can be shown on your phone at the stadium entrance.

Can I arrive after the first fight?

Yes. The gates normally stay open through the programme, so you can arrive after the first bout has started.

How early should I arrive?

The exact doors and first bout times are shown on each dated event page. Arriving before the first bout gives you time to get in and find your section without missing the opening action.

How long does a Muay Thai event last?

Running times vary by event. Check the dated event page for that night's schedule rather than assuming every Rajadamnern night finishes at the same time.

The venue

Rajadamnern Stadium, Bangkok

Rajadamnern opened in 1945 and remains one of Bangkok's historic homes of professional Muay Thai. It is on Ratchadamnoen Nok Road in central Bangkok, and it hosts a different promoter's fight night every night of the week.
