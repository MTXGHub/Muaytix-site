• Ringside

    Closest to the ring

    Floor level, sections 3 to 7. Close enough to hear the corner shouting instructions. Your seat number is printed on your ticket.

    See Ringside seating

  • Club Class

    Elevated view of the entire ring

    Sections 8 and 9, one tier up. You see the whole ring and the whole crowd. Your seat number is printed on your ticket, and a group booked in one order sits together.

    See Club Class seating

  • LEO Section

    Where the atmosphere lives

    Section 10, the same tier as Club Class. This is where the Thai regulars sit and where the noise comes from. Seating is open within the section.

    See the LEO Section

  • Third Class

    360 degree view of the action

    Section 11, the upper tier, and normally the lowest-priced seat on sale. Seating is open within the section. Third Class opens on selected nights only.

    See Third Class seating
