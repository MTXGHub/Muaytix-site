Plan your Rajadamnern Knockout night

Rajadamnern Knockout is held on Monday, Tuesday and Friday nights at Rajadamnern Stadium in Bangkok.

The event usually features seven three-round Muay Thai bouts. Doors open at 6:00 pm and the first fight begins at 7:00 pm.

Most Rajadamnern Knockout nights finish around 9:00 pm to 9:30 pm. Later timings can vary with the length of the bouts.
