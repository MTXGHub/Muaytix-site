What is All Star Fight by Buakaw?

All Star Fight by Buakaw is a monthly Muay Thai promotion presented by Buakaw Banchamek at Rajadamnern Stadium in Bangkok.

The event has its own identity within the weekly Rajadamnern programme, combining seven three-round contests with international matchmaking and a more globally focused presentation.

Buakaw is the promoter behind the event. He is not advertised as a fighter on the monthly Rajadamnern card.

The fighters change from month to month, so the confirmed card should always be checked on the relevant dated event page.

A monthly showcase of modern Muay Thai

All Star Fight is not simply another Monday programme.

The event is built around seven three-round contests, giving each fight a compact structure and encouraging fighters to establish themselves quickly.

Thai fighters regularly share the card with international opponents, giving the event a broader international character while retaining the rituals and fighting traditions of Muay Thai.

Wai Kru, traditional music and full Muay Thai rules remain part of the night, while the lighting and stadium presentation give the event a contemporary setting.

Because it normally appears only once each month, All Star Fight also has a different sense of occasion from Rajadamnern’s regular weekly programmes.

Thai and international fighters on one card

International matchmaking is an important part of All Star Fight.

Thai fighters regularly share the bill with opponents from other parts of Asia, Europe, the Middle East and the Americas.

Previous cards have included fighters representing countries including Thailand, Belarus, Morocco, Spain, France, Italy, Iran, Mexico, Germany, Russia, China and the United States.

The confirmed line-up changes for each event, so individual fighter names should only appear when the relevant fight card has been verified.

Seven fights, three rounds each

All Star Fight normally features seven professional Muay Thai bouts, with each contest scheduled for three rounds.

The shorter format keeps the programme moving and gives fighters less time to settle into the contest.

Decisions, knockouts and technical knockouts can all occur, but no individual result or knockout should ever be promised in advance.

Buakaw is the promoter, not one of the fighters

Buakaw Banchamek is one of the most recognisable figures in modern Muay Thai and kickboxing.

For All Star Fight, his role is as promoter and event creator, helping shape a monthly Rajadamnern event that connects Thai Muay Thai with an international audience.

All Star Fight by Buakaw questions

What is All Star Fight by Buakaw?

All Star Fight by Buakaw is a monthly Muay Thai promotion presented by Buakaw Banchamek at Rajadamnern Stadium in Bangkok. It normally features seven professional three-round bouts involving Thai and international fighters.

Is Buakaw fighting at All Star Fight?

No. Buakaw is the promoter behind the event. The fighters change from month to month and are listed on the individual dated event page once confirmed.

Is All Star Fight held every month?

It is scheduled as a monthly special event, normally on the last Monday of the month. Dates can change, so check the current booking calendar.

How many fights are on the card?

The standard format is seven bouts, with each bout scheduled for three rounds.

Is All Star Fight the same as Rajadamnern Knockout?

No. They use a similar three-round format, but All Star Fight is Buakaw’s branded monthly promotion and can replace the usual Monday Knockout programme.

Is All Star Fight suitable for someone new to Muay Thai?

The three-round format is straightforward to follow, while Wai Kru, traditional music and the stadium setting give first-time visitors an introduction to several important elements of Muay Thai.

Where is All Star Fight held?

All Star Fight is held at Rajadamnern Stadium on Ratchadamnoen Nok Road in Bangkok.
