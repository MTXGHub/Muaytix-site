What are Ringside seats like at Rajadamnern Stadium?

Ringside seats place you on the floor around the ring, only a few metres from the action.

The seats are assigned and located in Sections 3 to 7. You have a close view of the fighters and the ring, while corners, officials, cameras and production staff work nearby as part of the live fight-night environment.

Sections 3 and 4 are closest to the raised fighter entrance walkway.

Ringside is best suited to guests who value proximity, sound and the feeling of being inside the working event rather than watching from a distance.

Close enough to be part of the fight night

From Ringside, the ring is only a few metres away.

You see the fighters enter, watch the corners prepare between rounds and follow the officials and production team as they work around the ring.

You can hear the corner instructions at close range and see the concentration change as each bout develops.

The view is close, but Ringside is not separated from the rest of the event. The ropes, cameras, officials, team corners and movement around the ring all form part of what you are there to experience.

Where are the Ringside sections?

Ringside seating wraps around most of the ring in Sections 3 to 7.

The raised fighter entrance walkway runs between and through Sections 3 and 4. These sections are especially close to the formal fighter entrance route and have a smaller, more intimate layout than Sections 5, 6 and 7.

Fighters may leave by different routes after a bout, and movement around the ring can vary by fight, corner and event. Being close to fighters during entrances or after a bout should never be guaranteed.

Is front row the best Ringside seat?

Front row is the closest and most immersive Ringside position when available.

It is also the position most guests request, so MuayTix normally allocates front row first when it is available unless a guest asks for a different position.

There is no universal best Ringside row. Front row brings you closest to the ring and the surrounding activity, while other rows can offer a slightly wider perspective.

Guests in the front row may have a judge, production staff member or broadcast personnel positioned nearby. This is part of the live fight-night environment.

Front row is not guaranteed in advance.

Practical Ringside information

  • Food, soft drinks and beer are available for purchase and can be served directly to Ringside seats during the evening.

  • Personal photography and video recording are permitted.

  • Flash may be used between bouts, but not while a fight is taking place.

  • Commercial filming requires permission from the stadium in advance.

  • There is no formal smart-casual dress requirement. Dress sensibly for a sporting event inside an air-conditioned arena.
