What is Club Class at Rajadamnern Stadium?

Club Class is assigned seating in Sections 8 and 9, on the elevated tier of the main arena.

Every guest receives a numbered seat, making it easy to arrive, find your place and sit together as a group.

From Club Class, you look down across the ring and can follow the fighters, corners and movement across the canvas.

It is especially suitable for families, groups and first-time visitors who want a clear view without sitting on the floor beside the ring.

Club Class Sections 8 & 9

Club Class occupies Sections 8 and 9, positioned on opposite sides of the ring at Rajadamnern Stadium. This view shows exactly where both Club Class sections are located, with elevated seating and a clear perspective across the ring.

See the whole ring from above

Club Class looks down across the ring from the elevated main-arena tier.

The raised position gives you a broad view of the canvas, so it is easy to follow movement from one side of the ring to the other.

You can watch the fighters enter, see the corners working between rounds and take in more of the stadium around the ring.

MuayTix generally recommends Row D and above for the optimal viewing position. This brings you fully above the eye line of people moving along the walkway and creates a strong elevated perspective.

Rows A, B and C still provide a clear view of the ring. The row guidance is a preference, not a warning that the lower rows are poor seats.

A straightforward choice for families and groups

Club Class is MuayTix's most popular seating section, chosen by approximately 55% of guests.

It is particularly suitable for families with younger children because everyone has a numbered seat and the section is straightforward to navigate.

It also works well for groups who want to sit together without choosing an unassigned position on arrival.

Food, drinks, toilets and other facilities are easy to reach from the section, helping the evening run simply from arrival through to the final bout.

Simple seating, a clear view and less to think about

Club Class seats are simple blue plastic seat bases fixed onto the elevated tier.

They are not presented as padded or luxurious seats. The strength of Club Class is the position, the clear view and the ease of having a numbered seat.

You know where you are sitting before you arrive, and stadium staff can direct you to your position.

That makes Club Class a practical choice for visitors who want to spend the evening watching the Muay Thai rather than working out the seating arrangement.
