Three-round Muay Thai at Rajadamnern Stadium

Rajadamnern Knockout uses a three-round format that gives fighters less time to establish control and more reason to make each round count.

Seven bouts bring Thai and international fighters into the ring, while Wai Kru and live traditional Muay Thai music remain part of the evening.

Rajadamnern promotes the event with an advertised 80% knockout rate, giving the night a clear focus on decisive action without removing the traditions that identify the sport as Muay Thai.

Rajadamnern Knockout booking questions

What time does Rajadamnern Knockout start on 2 October 2026?

Doors open at 18:00 and the first fight begins at 19:00 Bangkok time.

How many fights are scheduled?

Rajadamnern Knockout is scheduled as seven three-round bouts.

What time will the event finish?

The event normally finishes at approximately 21:00 to 21:30. The exact finishing time can vary according to the fights on the night.

Can I arrive after the first fight?

Yes. The gates remain open during the evening, so guests may enter after the event has started.

Do I need to print my ticket?

No. The QR ticket can be shown on your phone when entering the stadium.

Will our seats be together?

Bookings of two or more assigned seats in one order are seated together. Unassigned seating categories do not have reserved individual seat positions.

Is Rajadamnern Knockout suitable if I have never watched Muay Thai?

The three-round format is straightforward to follow for a first live Muay Thai event. Wai Kru, traditional music and full Muay Thai rules remain part of the evening, while the shorter fight format keeps each contest compact.
