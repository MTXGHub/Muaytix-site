# MuayTix social media rules

Read this before writing or scheduling any social post. The house style in
`CLAUDE.md` (on branch `claude/new-agent-text-project-jyrrly`) also applies:
British English, no em dashes, no "official", no "Limited", facts only.

## Booking link

- Every post carries a booking link.
- The link goes in the **first comment**, never in the post text.
- Use the main tickets page, not day-dated pages:
  `https://muaytix.com/rajadamnern-stadium-tickets`
- End the post text with: `🎟️ Booking link in first comment`

## Schedule

| Post | Time (Bangkok) |
|---|---|
| Fight card, night before the fight | 21:00 |
| Fight card, on the day of the fight | 10:15 |

Facebook at the stated time, Instagram five minutes later.

## Fight cards

- Cards live in Google Drive, one folder per week.
- A card must say **TICKETS AT MUAYTIX.COM**. If it says rajadamnern.com,
  do not post it; ask Jason for the amended version.
- Facts come from the card or the booking database only. Never invent.
- Traditional nights (New Power, Petchyindee, Kiatpetch): the main card is the
  final four bouts, and the bout second from the end is usually the headline.
  Do not apply this to Knockout or RWS.

## Never put in a post

- Which nationalities are buying, or why a class is selling (for example
  Golden Week demand from China). Background only.
- Seat counts, unless Jason supplies the number for that post.
- Anything from customer ticket folders in Drive (names, bookings).
